Indian vs international numbering

10,00,000 (ten lakh)1,000,000 (one million)
1,00,00,000 (one crore)10,000,000 (ten million)

✍️ On a cheque, write the amount in words ending with “Only”, for example “Rupees Twenty Five Thousand Only”, so nobody can add to it.
