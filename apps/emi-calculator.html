How EMI is calculated

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months.

💡 Tips: a shorter tenure means a higher EMI but much less total interest. Paying even one extra EMI a year can cut years off a home loan. Rates shown are examples; check your bank's current rate.
