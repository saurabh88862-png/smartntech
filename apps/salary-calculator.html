How this is calculated

  • Gross salary = CTC − employer PF − gratuity (these two are part of CTC but not paid monthly).
  • In-hand = Gross salary − your PF (12% of basic) − professional tax − income tax.
  • Health & education cess of 4% is added to tax. Surcharge applies above ₹50 lakh income.

This is an estimate. Your company's salary structure (HRA, allowances, bonus, insurance) and state professional tax can change the exact figure.
