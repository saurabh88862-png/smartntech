Good to know

  • Translations come from the free MyMemory translation service, so your text is sent to it over the internet. Do not paste passwords, ID numbers or private documents.
  • There is a free daily limit (about 5,000 words per day per internet connection). If it is reached, try again tomorrow.
  • 🎤 Voice input works in Chrome and Edge. 🔊 Listening uses your device's voices; for Hindi, install a Hindi voice if you hear nothing.
  • Machine translation can make mistakes. Check important text (legal, medical) with a person who knows the language.
