How GST is calculated

➕ Add GST

GST = Amount × Rate ÷ 100. Total = Amount + GST.
Example: ₹1,000 at 18% → GST ₹180 → Total ₹1,180.

➖ Remove GST

Original = Total × 100 ÷ (100 + Rate). GST = Total − Original.
Example: ₹1,180 incl. 18% → Original ₹1,000, GST ₹180.

🏠 Same state

GST is split equally: half CGST (Centre) and half SGST (State). 18% = 9% CGST + 9% SGST.

🚚 Other state

The full GST is charged as IGST (Integrated GST). 18% = 18% IGST.

Main slabs after the September 2025 GST reform are 5% and 18%, with 40% for luxury and sin goods; 0.25% and 3% apply to stones and gold. Older 12% and 28% rates are kept for past invoices. Always check the current rate for your item.
