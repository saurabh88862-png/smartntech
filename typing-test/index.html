How WPM is counted

Every 5 correct characters (spaces included) count as one word. Wrong characters do not count.

🎯

What is a good speed?

30–40 WPM is average, 50–60 is good for office work, 70+ is excellent. Aim for 95%+ accuracy first.

🏋️

Practise daily

Ten minutes a day with all ten fingers and without looking at the keys raises speed fastest.
